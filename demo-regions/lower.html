Operational Context

The Lower Danube receives cumulative upstream pressures while supporting critical wetlands and delta habitats. The package combines river restoration with nature-based adaptation to hydrological extremes.

  • Reconnect cut-off floodplain sectors to improve sediment and nutrient exchange.
  • Restore secondary distributary pathways to reduce local erosion/deposition imbalances.
  • Stabilize priority wetland edges with low-impact bioengineering approaches.
  • Coordinate restoration windows with fisheries and biodiversity protection calendars.
