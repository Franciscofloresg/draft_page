Operational Context

The Middle Danube combines regulated reaches and active navigation corridors with high ecological value. The regional package aligns river engineering, navigation safety, and ecosystem restoration targets.

  • Reopen floodplain inlets and side branches to restore lateral connectivity.
  • Optimize dredging/disposal practices to preserve sediment within the active corridor.
  • Stabilize selected banks using nature-based techniques to reduce hard confinement.
  • Implement joint cross-border monitoring protocols for hydromorphological indicators.
