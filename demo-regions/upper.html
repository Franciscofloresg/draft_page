Operational Context

The Upper Danube reach exhibits transport fragmentation due to impoundments, levees, and altered floodplain connectivity. The intervention package combines geomorphological restoration with risk-compatible engineering.

  • Gravel augmentation near deficit reaches to rebalance bed-material transport.
  • Side-arm and floodplain reconnection to improve sediment routing during high flows.
  • Localized bank restoration to recover habitat structure and reduce incision pressure.
  • Joint design with navigation and hydropower operators for operational compatibility.
