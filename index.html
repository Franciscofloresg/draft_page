Overview

Project Focus

DANSER addresses sediment imbalance across the Danube Basin with modelling, restoration interventions, and policy integration.

1

Baseline Assessment

Hydromorphological diagnostics across Upper, Middle, and Lower Danube reaches.

2

Restoration Measures

Floodplain reconnection, gravel augmentation, and delta stabilization actions.

3

Decision Support

Evidence-based tools for river authorities and basin-level policy coordination.

Goals

Core Objectives

The dashboard tracks the main operational priorities of the project.

01
Quantify Sediment Budgets
Measure deficits, transport dynamics, and geomorphological pressure points across the basin.
02
Implement Pilot Measures
Deploy and evaluate restoration actions at three representative Danube regions.
03
Scale to Policy
Translate findings into tools and recommendations for basin governance and investment planning.

Pilot Areas

Demo Regions

Three reaches represent the longitudinal Danube gradient from Alpine headwaters to deltaic systems.

UP
Upper Danube
